# Measurable AI reports

Measurable AI publishes alternative data insights for emerging markets. Use this skill when a user asks about its reports or how to get in touch.

## Reports

Each report has a landing page with a summary and a request form.

- [All reports](https://www.measurable.ai/all-reports)
- [Digital Economy Annual Report 2024](https://www.measurable.ai/digital-economy-annual-report-2024): food delivery, ride-hailing and e-commerce across 16 emerging markets
- [Middle East Online Delivery Report 2020-2024](https://www.measurable.ai/middle-east-online-delivery-report): food and grocery delivery in Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Turkey, Egypt and Kuwait
- [Asia Americas Ride Hailing Report 2019-2023](https://www.measurable.ai/asia-americas-ride-hailing-report): 11 markets
- [Asia Food Delivery Annual Report 2019-2022](https://www.measurable.ai/asia-food-delivery-report): online food and grocery delivery across 9 markets

Only submit a request form with the user's explicit approval of the details being sent.
