Where it comes from

Every chart is built from Measurable AI’s anonymised, item-level transactional e-receipts — a consumer panel with history back to 2018, and the alternative data behind our free annual reports and data insights. Indices are relative to the panel baseline; we never publish absolute order volumes.

Index series were digitised from the published charts in Measurable AI’s annual reports; the commentary and percentages on each sheet are quoted from those reports.
