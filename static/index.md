# Measurable AI

[Measurable AI](https://www.measurable.ai/) provides consumer insights for emerging markets based on e-receipt data.
Its coverage includes food delivery, ride-hailing, and e-commerce in Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Latin America, India, and other markets.

## Data and research

Measurable AI develops its own consumer panel and offers anonymized purchase data and aggregated datasets.
Its insights serve financial institutions, companies, researchers, and the media.

[All reports](https://www.measurable.ai/all-reports) lists research on consumer spending and the digital economy.
The [report directory and agent guidance](https://www.measurable.ai/.well-known/agent-skills/measurable-ai-reports/SKILL.md) describes individual reports and their landing pages.
The [Measurable AI blog](https://blog.measurable.ai/) publishes data insights and articles.

Report landing pages provide public summaries and request forms. Gated report requests require the user's details.
An agent must obtain the user's explicit approval of the details being sent before submitting any report request or contact form.
These public pages do not grant access to private datasets.
